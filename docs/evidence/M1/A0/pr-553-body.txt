## What

Feature **A0**: `omos-ingest`, the collector framework the ingestion lane recommended (plain Go on `nats.go`, in `services/bridge/ingest`). The first migrated feed is **NWS alerts**, the life-safety feed. The legacy `feed-intel` NOAA task is **unchanged and keeps running** beside it until the new pipeline matches it for 7 days (decision 0004 #18).

- **One owner per source.** KV lease `ingest-locks`. It survives a bus outage, and a crashed leader is replaced within the TTL.
- **Acked publish.** Synchronous JetStream publish with retries and `Nats-Msg-Id` dedup. KV checkpoints (`ingest-state`) are written only after acks. A failed checkpoint read skips the poll.
- **Schema validation and dead letters.** Records are checked against JSON Schemas in `services/schemas`. Bad records go to `evt.sys.{source}.rejected` with the reason.
- **Upstream politeness.** Per-source rate limit and quota, ETag and If-Modified-Since, Retry-After, and backoff on 429 and 5xx. 403 is not retried. Every source must record its terms of use.
- **Freshness.** `tel.feed.{source}.status` carries `fetchedAt`, record count and last error. Matching `ingest_feed_*` Prometheus metrics feed a stale-feed alert. Logs are JSON. Memory is bounded.
- **Delivery modes.** REST poll, RSS/Atom, file/bulk (`-once` for a CronJob), SSE (resumes with `Last-Event-ID`) and WebSocket (bounded queue that drops the oldest).
